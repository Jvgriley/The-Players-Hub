About Us

Building a Platform for
Grassroots Football

We want to achieve a platform for players in grassroots — the developmental pathways to elite football. The Players Hub is more than a training centre; it is a community built on opportunity.

The Players Hub provides a safe space for players of all ages to develop confidence, technical ability, discipline and opportunity. From first steps on the pitch to pathways into elite football — we are here to support every stage of a player's journey.

Meet The Founder

Michael Cooper

Founder & Director, The Players Hub

Michael Cooper founded The Players Hub with one clear goal: to create better opportunities, structure and development pathways for young footballers in North London.

Having seen first-hand the talent that exists at grassroots level — and the lack of structured pathways available to many young players — Michael built The Players Hub to bridge that gap. His vision is to grow this development centre into a full club, with teams at every age group, and genuine routes into elite football for every player who comes through the door.

At The Players Hub, every player is treated as an individual, given the support, structure and belief they need to achieve their potential. Michael is committed to building a programme that families can trust.

The Journey

Your Development Pathway

A structured journey from grassroots foundations to elite football opportunities — every step designed with the player in mind.

01

Grassroots Player Development

Every great player starts somewhere. Our grassroots programme gives beginners and developing players the fundamentals they need — in a safe, supportive and structured environment.

02

Technical Training

Coach-led sessions focused on real technical improvement: ball mastery, passing, movement, positioning and game intelligence — building the skills that separate good players from great ones.

03

Confidence & Discipline

We develop the full player — not just their feet. Through structured sessions and a positive environment, players build mental resilience, self-belief, and the discipline that defines top performers.

Showcase Games

Showcase matches are a core part of the development pathway at The Players Hub. These carefully organised games give players the platform to demonstrate their ability in a competitive yet supportive environment — helping them gain visibility, match experience and the confidence that only real game-time can provide.

Showcase events are designed to attract the attention of scouts, coaches and academies, opening doors that might otherwise remain closed for grassroots talent.

Real match experience in a structured setting
Visibility for scouts & academy coaches
Builds confidence through competitive play
Video & performance feedback opportunities
Family-friendly showcase events
05

Future Club Pathway

As The Players Hub grows, we will build competitive teams at every age group. Players who develop with us will have the opportunity to represent The Players Hub FC when the time comes.

06

Elite Football Opportunities

Our ultimate aim is to open doors to elite football. Through our showcase events, coaching network and development records, we aim to give talented players the best possible chance of reaching the highest levels.
